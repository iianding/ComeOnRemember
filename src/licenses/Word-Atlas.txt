ComeOnRemember selects British and American phonetics and examples, supplies target highlights and associates them
with vocabulary entries. Rows marked 搭配改编 use an original context written
around a source collocation. Rows marked 同义语境改编 adapt a source sentence
for a related target word. The adapted text retains the same attribution and
license. No source images or recordings are included.
